What this is

A proof-of-concept research framework using a physically motivated reduced-order model combining a Rankine vortex, pulse-train blade signature, and Dryden atmospheric turbulence.

Important scope: This is not a field-validated detection system and not a full 3D CFD solve. Full CFD is identified in the paper as the necessary next step; it is not included in this package.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a validated, deployable detection system?

No. It is a proof-of-concept research framework and should not be represented as field-validated or deployment-ready.

What corrections were made during review?

The Complete package includes a README documenting the two corrections applied during review.

Why is 3-class accuracy only 68.9%?

The result is reported as obtained rather than presented as a guarantee. The three classes are multirotor, fixed-wing, and bird under the modeled conditions.

Do I need CFD software?

No. The supplied model is reduced-order and uses Python libraries such as NumPy, SciPy, and scikit-learn. Full CFD is discussed as a next research step.

Is this suitable for a graduate thesis or publication?

The materials can be used as a research starting point, subject to your institution's rules, your own analysis, and appropriate citation. No publication or thesis outcome is guaranteed.
